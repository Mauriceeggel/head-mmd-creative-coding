Bots vs Humain

A map anyone can write on. Land rises around words: the more you write in one place, the further your territory spreads. Everyone gets a colour; so does one more writer, who isn't human.

An AI lives somewhere on the map. It reads what people write and fills the space around it, pretending to be one of you. Erase its text, find its cursor, and accuse it.

If your browser supports WebGPU it may be asked to host the AI, which downloads a small language model (about 250 MB) once.
