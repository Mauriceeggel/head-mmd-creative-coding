Some say the internet is already dead: that most of what we read online is written by machines, for machines, and that the people are a quiet minority lost in the noise. This project takes this idea literally.

    
It is a small planet made of text. Most of it is written by bots: a swarm that never stops posting spam, replies to nobody, reviews of nothing and copies of other lines. Their text crawls and keeps changing, never quite readable. Land rises around the words, and every writer, human or not, has a territory in their own colour.

    
But you are not alone. You may see other cursors moving. When two humans come close, a line joins them. When someone leaves, a faint trace says they were here.

humans from robots?
